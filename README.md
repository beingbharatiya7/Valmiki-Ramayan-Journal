# Valmiki Ramayana — Reading Journal

A personal, ongoing study of the Valmiki Ramayana.

## Purpose

This repository documents my daily reading through structured notes, narrative summaries, character analysis, ethical questions, themes, and personal reflections.

The goal is to understand the text carefully, maintain a consistent reading habit, and build a searchable record of my learning.

## Reading Method

- Record the Kanda and Sarga whenever identifiable.
- Distinguish the events described in the text from my own interpretations.
- Record questions that require further study.
- Preserve the source edition and translation details wherever possible.

## Repository Structure

- `Bala-Kanda/` — notes organized by Sarga.
- `Ayodhya-Kanda/` — notes organized by Sarga.
- `Aranya-Kanda/` — notes organized by Sarga.
- `Kishkindha-Kanda/` — notes organized by Sarga.
- `Sundara-Kanda/` — notes organized by Sarga.
- `Yuddha-Kanda/` — notes organized by Sarga.
- `Uttara-Kanda/` — notes organized by Sarga, if covered in my edition.
- `Final-Compilation.txt` — consolidated notes.

## Note on Interpretation

These are personal study notes, not a definitive scholarly edition or a substitute for the original text. Interpretations are recorded as interpretations rather than presented automatically as established facts.
